Existing policy review

Understand current coverage before considering a change.

A policy review can help you organize what you own, what is guaranteed, what can change, and whether the coverage still aligns with your goals. A review is not an instruction to replace or surrender a policy.

i

Important to know

Do not cancel existing coverage until any new coverage has been approved, accepted, delivered, and is in force. Replacements may involve new underwriting, surrender charges, changed guarantees, and new periods when an insurer may review application statements or apply the policy’s suicide exclusion.

What to bring

Use the insurer’s current annual statement, policy contract, in-force illustration when applicable, beneficiary information, and notes about your present goals. Do not upload policy numbers or personal records through this website.

What to examine

Review premiums, death benefit, cash value, loans, riders, beneficiaries, guarantees, non-guaranteed assumptions, surrender charges, and lapse risk. Questions should be directed to the issuing insurer when contract details are unclear.

Replacement requires formal safeguards

If a replacement is eventually considered, the insurer, producer, and applicant must follow carrier and state-required notices and procedures. This website does not perform a replacement or accept instructions about an existing policy.
