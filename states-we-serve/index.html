Availability has more than one layer.

An agency record alone does not establish that every producer can respond in a state or that every carrier and product is available. Before a specific conversation, verify:

  • The exact legal entity and approved trade-name relationship
  • The responding producer’s active Life line of authority
  • Carrier appointment and product availability for the state
  • Any required state or carrier advertising and disclosure review

Do not submit an inquiry from a state not listed in the form. The website does not create an office or physical location in any state.
