How it works

A straightforward path from questions to an informed decision.

Important to know

Submitting an inquiry is not an insurance application and does not create coverage. Only an issuing insurer can approve an application and issue a policy.

1. Make a general inquiry

Tell us your state, broad topic, and preferred way to be contacted. Do not send medical, financial-account, Social Security, or policy-number information.

2. Confirm who may assist

Before product discussion, the responding producer should confirm licensing and applicable product availability for your state.

3. Compare suitable options

A conversation may cover coverage purpose, time horizon, budget, existing coverage, policy differences, and questions for the insurer. Any illustration must come from current approved carrier materials.

4. Apply through the insurer

If you choose to apply, underwriting information should be submitted through the insurer’s authorized process. The insurer decides eligibility, classification, premium, and issuance.

5. Review before accepting

Read the issued policy, premium, riders, exclusions, the free-look period when the owner may return the policy under its terms, and any differences from the application or illustration before accepting coverage.
