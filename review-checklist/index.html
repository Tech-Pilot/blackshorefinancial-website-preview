Design decisions

Visual feedback to provide

These are preference and usability decisions. Review both desktop and mobile views.

  • Logo: Is its size, placement, and prominence right? Is the lighthouse mark legible?
  • Color: Does the coastal palette feel trustworthy and appropriate for the brand?
  • Typography: Are headings distinctive and is body copy comfortable to read?
  • Flow: Can you quickly understand what the firm does, how the process works, and where to learn more?
  • Calls to action: Is “Request a Consultation” the right phrase, and is it placed often enough without feeling pushy?
  • Content: Flag anything unclear, too technical, repetitive, missing, or inconsistent with how Blackshore should sound.

Launch blockers

Facts and approvals still required

These items are deliberately omitted or described as unverified until documentary evidence and the appropriate approval are recorded.

  • Identity and licensing: Exact legal entity and approved DBA disclosure; agency and individual producer identities, NPNs, licenses, lines of authority, evidence, and expiration dates.
  • State, carrier, and product availability: Approved service states, producer routing, carrier appointments, product availability by state, and required carrier advertising review.
  • Contact details: Verified phone number, email addresses, physical-address decision, business hours, timezone, and successful call/email testing.
  • Privacy and data handling: Actual hosting, email, lead-delivery, CRM, and form-security providers; recipient access; retention, deletion, sensitive-message, rate-limit, incident, and privacy-rights procedures.
  • Legal and carrier approval: Final privacy notice, terms, solicitation disclosure, accessibility statement, advertising copy, and documented owner/counsel/carrier sign-off where applicable.
  • Production deployment: Approved CloudPanel access, HTTPS and security headers, transactional email, monitoring, backups, restore rehearsal, rollback readiness, and explicit launch authorization.
  • Accessibility verification: Final keyboard, zoom, screen-reader, browser/device, and any third-party component testing after the production configuration is known.

Suggested pass

How to review this preview

  1. Start at the homepage and follow the navigation as a first-time visitor.
  2. Review the life-insurance education pages for clarity and tone.
  3. Open the Contact page to inspect the planned fields and disclosure; the form cannot submit.
  4. Send visual/content notes separately from verified business facts and approval evidence.
