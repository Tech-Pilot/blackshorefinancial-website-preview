Design decisions

Visual feedback to provide

These are preference and usability decisions. Review both desktop and mobile views.

  • Logo: Is its size, placement, and prominence right? Is the lighthouse mark legible?
  • Color: Does the coastal palette feel trustworthy and appropriate for the brand?
  • Typography: Are headings distinctive and is body copy comfortable to read?
  • Flow: Can you quickly understand what the firm does, how the process works, and where to learn more?
  • Calls to action: Is “Request a Consultation” the right phrase, and is it placed often enough without feeling pushy?
  • Content: Flag anything unclear, too technical, repetitive, missing, or inconsistent with how Blackshore should sound.

Launch blockers

Facts and approvals still required

These items separate completed owner and regulator facts from the operational, agency, carrier/product, and routing evidence still required before launch.

  • Identity and licensing: The Minnesota LLC record and Nicholas Cherry’s NPN and six individual Life-authority records are verified. Blackshore LLC agency authority, agency NPN, affiliation, and deterministic lead routing remain required.
  • State, carrier, and product availability: Carrier appointments or authorization, product availability by state, producer routing, and any future carrier/product-specific advertising review remain separate gates.
  • Contact details: Phone, email, published Minnesota business address, hours, Central Time, after-hours voicemail, manual-only callbacks, and the Facebook link are owner-approved. Successful call and email route tests remain open.
  • Privacy and data handling: Gmail SMTP and first-party form controls are selected, and the 60-day inquiry schedule, one-business-day sensitive-content deletion procedure, MFA-protected Gmail recipient, and accessibility response channel are owner-approved. Protected credential installation, delivery/deletion propagation testing, rate-limit, incident, and privacy-rights operations remain required.
  • Review scope: The owner’s legal/carrier-review attestation is accepted for the current generic website without external reviewer metadata or any public external-approval claim. A future carrier/product-specific claim or material scope change requires a new scoped review.
  • Production deployment: The owner selected a direct-origin rollout with the incumbent DNS left live during testing. HTTPS and security headers, Gmail SMTP delivery, monitoring, backup restore evidence, rollback readiness, and explicit authorization for the origin-tested release hash remain required before cutover.
  • Accessibility verification: Final keyboard, zoom, screen-reader, browser/device, and any third-party component testing after the production configuration is known.
