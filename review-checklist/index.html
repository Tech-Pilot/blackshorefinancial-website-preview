Design decisions

Visual feedback to provide

These are preference and usability decisions. Review both desktop and mobile views.

  • Logo: Is its size, placement, and prominence right? Is the lighthouse mark legible?
  • Color: Does the coastal palette feel trustworthy and appropriate for the brand?
  • Typography: Are headings distinctive and is body copy comfortable to read?
  • Flow: Can you quickly understand what the firm does, how the process works, and where to learn more?
  • Calls to action: Is “Request a Consultation” the right phrase, and is it placed often enough without feeling pushy?
  • Content: Flag anything unclear, too technical, repetitive, missing, or inconsistent with how Blackshore should sound.

Launch blockers

Facts and approvals still required

These items are deliberately omitted or described as unverified until documentary evidence and the appropriate approval are recorded.

  • Identity and licensing: The Minnesota LLC record is verified. Agency and individual producer identities, NPNs, licenses, lines of authority, evidence, and expiration dates remain required.
  • State, carrier, and product availability: Approved service states, producer routing, carrier appointments, product availability by state, and required carrier advertising review.
  • Contact details: Phone, email, published Minnesota business address, hours, Central Time, after-hours voicemail, manual-only callbacks, and the Facebook link are owner-approved. Successful call and email route tests remain open.
  • Privacy and data handling: The 60-day inquiry schedule, one-business-day sensitive-content deletion procedure, MFA-protected Gmail recipient, and accessibility response channel are owner-approved. Actual sending/form-security providers, deletion propagation testing, rate-limit, incident, and privacy-rights operations remain required.
  • Legal and carrier approval: Record the reviewer, date, exact release, states/products/carriers, conditions, and approval reference for the reported completed reviews. Producer and carrier evidence must match that scope.
  • Production deployment: A separate protected staging hostname/path, HTTPS and security headers, transactional email, monitoring, off-site backup and restore rehearsal, rollback readiness, and explicit launch authorization remain required. The supplied production document root is not used for review deployment.
  • Accessibility verification: Final keyboard, zoom, screen-reader, browser/device, and any third-party component testing after the production configuration is known.
