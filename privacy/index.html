Privacy notice

How this website handles information.

Draft date: August 2, 2026

!

Pre-launch legal review required

Lead collection and optional tracking remain disabled while the exact legal entity, vendors, retention practices, state-law analysis, and request process are finalized and reviewed by qualified counsel.

1. Scope

This notice describes information handled through the Blackshore Financial website, including a consultation request submitted directly on the site. It does not describe an insurer’s application or underwriting systems, which have their own notices and safeguards.

This website is designed to avoid collecting health information, Social Security numbers, financial-account data, policy numbers, and other underwriting information.

2. Information collected

Information you provide

If consultation requests are activated, the form collects first and last name, email address, phone number, state of residence, broad insurance topic, contact preference and window, an optional general message, and validated campaign/source fields.

Information collected automatically

The hosting and security systems may process technical information such as an IP address, request time, browser or device information, requested path, and security signals to deliver the site, protect forms, enforce request limits, and diagnose operational failures. Application logs are designed to record request identifiers and operational status without form contents.

Other sources

If Meta Instant Forms are later approved and used, the person may submit contact information directly to Meta. Blackshore would receive only the approved fields, subject to Meta’s notice and the campaign disclosure. That workflow is not active through this website today.

3. How information is used

  • Respond to a requested insurance-related inquiry
  • Confirm the responding producer’s state authority and relevant availability
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website
  • Honor privacy choices and requests
  • Maintain records required for authorized business, compliance, or legal purposes

The website does not accept an insurance application, bind coverage, or make an underwriting decision.

4. Service providers and disclosures

Approved vendors may process information on Blackshore’s instructions for website hosting, security, transactional message delivery, lead management, analytics, or advertising measurement. Before production collection begins, this notice must identify the actual hosting, lead-delivery, CRM, email, and form-security providers in use and state the approved sale, sharing, and targeted-advertising determinations.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and security, respond to lawful process, or support a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

When a person chooses to apply, information should be sent through an approved insurer’s process. The insurer determines its own collection, underwriting, retention, and privacy practices.

5. Retention and security

Consultation information should be retained only for an approved period tied to responding, compliance, and legitimate recordkeeping, then deleted or de-identified under a documented process. The final period and deletion workflow must be approved before lead collection is activated.

Safeguards are designed to include encrypted transport, restricted access, protected environment secrets, form validation, CSRF protection, request limiting, bot defenses, and operational logging that excludes form contents. No system can promise absolute security.

6. Cookies, analytics, and advertising technology

This public review writes no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising integrations. Any production cookie use remains subject to documented approval and testing.

Production consent controls are not active in this preview. If optional technologies are later proposed, consent and Global Privacy Control behavior must be approved and tested before activation.

GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, and Conversions API are prepared only as future possibilities and are not loaded by this build while disabled. Form fields, messages, health information, and lead contact data must never be sent as analytics or advertising event parameters.

Review cookie preferences.

7. Privacy choices and requests

Depending on residence and applicable law, a person may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or information about disclosures, and to appeal certain decisions. Some information may be exempt or need to be retained as permitted by law.

No production privacy-rights request channel is active in this public review draft. Do not send a privacy request through the sales consultation form. A dedicated, minimized request method, verification process, response deadlines, and appeal tracking must be approved before launch.

Qualified counsel must determine whether insurance-specific privacy rules, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, or state privacy laws apply to a particular data flow.

8. Contact

A dedicated, verified privacy contact method is required before public launch. The sales consultation form is not a privacy-rights channel.

This notice will be revised when material data practices or providers change. The revision date at the top will be updated.
