1. Scope

This notice describes information handled through the Blackshore Financial website, the listed email address, and a consultation request submitted directly on the site after that feature is activated. It does not describe an insurer’s application or underwriting systems, which have their own notices, records, and safeguards.

Blackshore Financial refers to Blackshore financial L.L.C., a Minnesota domestic limited liability company (File No. 1626850500022).

This website is designed to avoid collecting health information, Social Security numbers, financial-account data, policy numbers, beneficiary details, and other underwriting information.

2. Information collected

Information you provide

Email inquiries may include the sender’s email address and whatever nonsensitive content the sender chooses to provide. If online consultation requests are activated, the form collects first and last name, email address and/or phone number, state of residence, broad insurance topic, contact preference and window, an optional general message, and approved campaign/source fields.

Information collected automatically

Hosting and security systems may process technical information such as an IP address, request time, browser or device information, requested path, response status, and security signals to deliver the site, protect forms, enforce request limits, and diagnose failures. Application logs are designed to record request identifiers and operational status without form contents.

Other sources

If Meta Instant Forms are later approved and used, a person may submit contact information directly to Meta. That workflow is not active through this website today and would require an updated data-flow review and notice before activation.

3. How information is used

  • Respond manually by phone or email to the requested insurance-related inquiry
  • Confirm the responding producer’s state authority and relevant availability
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website
  • Honor privacy, stop-contact, and do-not-call requests
  • Maintain records required for authorized business, compliance, or legal purposes

The website does not accept an insurance application, bind coverage, make an underwriting decision, or enroll a visitor in SMS, automated dialing, prerecorded calls, artificial-voice calls, or call recording.

4. Service providers and disclosures

Providers selected for this website are: hosting — CloudPanel-hosted NGINX and PHP-FPM server; lead delivery — Google Gmail SMTP; CRM — No CRM selected; email — Google Gmail; and form security — First-party controls.

Inquiry information may be disclosed to authorized Blackshore personnel assigned to respond to the inquiry and approved service providers needed to deliver that response for responding to the requested inquiry, confirming who may lawfully assist, securing the website, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Blackshore financial L.L.C. does not sell personal information, does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not use inquiry information for targeted advertising under the practices adopted for this website.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and security, respond to lawful process, or support a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

If a person later chooses to apply, application and underwriting information should be sent through an approved insurer’s process. The insurer determines its own collection, use, disclosure, retention, and privacy practices.

5. 60-day inquiry retention and security

Adopted inquiry schedule: Blackshore’s copy of website and direct-email consultation inquiry content is scheduled for deletion no later than 60 calendar days after receipt. Records created later in an insurer application, policy transaction, legal hold, complaint, or legally required compliance process are separate and follow their applicable retention rules.

Accidentally submitted sensitive information: Blackshore’s procedure is to restrict access and permanently delete that content from Blackshore-controlled active and staff-accessible systems, including Gmail Trash, within one business day after receipt. The workflow also propagates deletion to every approved recipient and provider that holds an accessible copy.

Safeguards are designed to include encrypted transport, restricted account access, multifactor authentication where supported, protected environment secrets, form validation, CSRF protection, request limiting, bot defenses, and operational logging that excludes inquiry contents. No system can promise absolute security.

6. Cookies, analytics, and advertising technology

Essential cookies may support form security, session continuity, and stored privacy choices on the eventual production site. Optional analytics and advertising integrations remain disabled in this build.

The Facebook link in the footer is an ordinary external link; this build does not embed a Facebook widget, Meta Pixel, or Conversions API. Following the link takes the visitor to Facebook, whose own terms and privacy practices apply.

Form fields, messages, health information, and lead contact data must never be sent as analytics or advertising event parameters. If optional technology is proposed later, this notice and the consent controls must be reviewed and updated before activation.

Review cookie preferences.

7. Privacy choices and requests

Depending on residence and applicable law, a person may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or information about disclosures, and to appeal certain decisions. Some insurance, legal, security, or compliance records may be exempt or retained as permitted or required by law.

Send a privacy request to blackshorefinancial@gmail.com. State the request type and state of residence, and do not include account, policy, health, financial, or government-identification details in the first message. To appeal a response: Reply to the privacy response or email blackshorefinancial@gmail.com with “Privacy Appeal” in the subject line. Blackshore will evaluate requests under the law that applies to the requester and the information involved; insurance and legally required records may be subject to exceptions. Identity verification may be required through a safe, proportionate process.

Blackshore will honor a request to stop manual follow-up. Insurance-specific privacy rules, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, and state privacy laws may apply differently depending on the information and activity involved; Blackshore will evaluate the actual request and update this notice when its practices materially change.
