Our approach

Blackshore Financial is working toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. Accessibility is treated as part of design, development, content review, and ongoing maintenance—not as an overlay.

Features in this website

  • Semantic landmarks and a consistent heading structure
  • A skip link and full keyboard access
  • Persistent visible focus and minimum-size touch targets
  • Labeled form fields, instructions, error summaries, and status messages
  • Responsive reflow and text resizing support
  • Reduced-motion support and restrained animation
  • Color contrast designed to meet WCAG AA

Known limitations and ongoing review

Accessibility testing is ongoing across browsers, keyboard use, zoom levels, responsive widths, and assistive technologies. Third-party services, if later approved, may introduce experiences that Blackshore does not fully control and must be assessed before activation.

Report an accessibility barrier

No public accessibility-report channel is active in this public review draft. A dedicated, verified accessibility contact and response timeframe must be approved before public launch; the sales consultation form is not used for barrier reports.
