Common questions

Open any question below. If your question involves an existing policy, contact the issuing insurer before making a change.

What does life insurance do?

Life insurance can pay a death benefit to the policy’s beneficiary after a covered death while the policy is in force. The beneficiary may use proceeds according to their needs, subject to the policy and applicable law.

Is submitting this website form an application?

No. The form is only a request to be contacted. An insurance application, underwriting, approval, and policy issuance occur through an authorized insurer process.

Is coverage guaranteed?

No. Eligibility, classification, premium, coverage, and available products depend on the insurer, policy, state, age, health, and underwriting.

Does term life build cash value?

Term life generally does not build cash value. It is designed to provide coverage for a stated period when the policy remains in force.

Can permanent life insurance lapse?

Yes. A permanent policy can lapse if required premiums or policy values are insufficient to cover charges, among other reasons. Flexible premiums and an index-crediting floor do not eliminate lapse risk.

Is indexed universal life an investment in the stock market?

No. It is life insurance. A policy may use an external index in an interest-crediting formula, but the policyholder does not directly own index shares or receive the index’s full return.

Are policy loans tax-free?

Tax treatment depends on the policy and circumstances. Loans and withdrawals can reduce policy value and death benefit, increase lapse risk, and may have tax consequences. Consult a qualified tax professional for tax advice.

Is mortgage protection required by my lender?

No. The life insurance discussed on this site is not private mortgage insurance, is not required by a lender, and is not affiliated with a lender or government agency.

Should I cancel current coverage before applying for new coverage?

No. Existing coverage should not be cancelled until new coverage is approved, accepted, delivered, and in force. A replacement can have significant costs and consequences.

What information should I put in the contact form?

Share only basic contact details, your state, and a broad topic. Do not include Social Security numbers, medical details, account or policy numbers, or other sensitive information.

Does this website provide tax, legal, or investment advice?

No. Website content is general insurance education. Consult appropriately qualified professionals for tax, legal, investment, or estate-planning advice.

When does coverage begin?

Submitting an inquiry does not begin coverage. Coverage starts only according to the issuing insurer’s approval, delivery, acceptance, premium, and policy requirements.
